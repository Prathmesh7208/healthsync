# Phase 3 — Scope and Objectives

**Module Prefix:** `P3-SCP`
**Priority:** P3 — Future
**Status:** Draft

---

## 1. Objectives

- Leverage AI/ML for early risk detection and chronic disease monitoring.
- Digitize health insurance claims for cashless consultations and admissions.
- Support multi-department hospital operations (IPD, Bed Tracking, OT).
- Integrate continuous vital monitoring from IoT wearables.
- Ensure 100% interoperability with India's Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM).
