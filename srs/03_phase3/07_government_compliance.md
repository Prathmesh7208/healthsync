## 7.1 Overview

Integrates HealthSync with the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) sandbox/production gateways to enable creation of ABHA IDs, registration as a Health Information Provider (HIP) and Health Information User (HIU), and interoperable record exchange over FHIR standards.
