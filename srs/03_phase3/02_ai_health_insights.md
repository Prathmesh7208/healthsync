# Phase 3 — AI Health Insights

**Module Prefix:** `AIH`
**Priority:** P3 — Future
**Status:** Draft

---

## 2.1 Overview

Applies machine learning models to anonymized patient health history to detect medication non-adherence, flag potential drug interactions, and generate personalized wellness recommendations.

---

## 2.2 Functional Requirements

- **Medication Adherence Engine:** Analyzes prescription refills and sends intelligent reminders.
- **Drug Interaction Checker:** Real-time warnings during doctor prescription entry for conflicting medications.
- **Risk Score Analytics:** Predicts chronic disease flare-ups based on historical vital trends.
