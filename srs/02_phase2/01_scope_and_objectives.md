# Phase 2 — Scope and Objectives

**Module Prefix:** `P2-SCP`
**Priority:** P1 — Must Have
**Status:** Draft

---

## 1. Phase 2 Objectives

1. **Enable Telemedicine:** Provide remote consultation capabilities for patients unable to visit clinics physically.
2. **Monetize Platform Operations:** Integrate digital payment channels to support consultation fee collection, subscription models, and instant doctor payouts.
3. **Expand Service Ecosystem:** Connect pharmacy partners for home medicine delivery and diagnostic labs for home sample collection.
4. **Support Family Healthcare:** Allow primary account holders to manage healthcare, appointments, and records for family members and dependents.

---

## 2. In-Scope Modules

- Tele-consultation via high-definition WebRTC/Agora video.
- Payment Gateway integration (Razorpay / UPI / Cards / NetBanking).
- E-Pharmacy partner API integration.
- Diagnostic lab sample booking and report sync.
- Dependent family profile management under single HealthSync primary ID.
- Doctor earnings dashboard & automated weekly settlement.
