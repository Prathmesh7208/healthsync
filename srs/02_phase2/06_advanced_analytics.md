# Phase 2 — Advanced Analytics Module

**Module Prefix:** `ANL`
**Priority:** P2 — Should Have (Phase 2)
**Status:** Draft

---

## 6.1 Purpose

Provides clinic administrators and doctors with deep operational insights, patient retention metrics, peak queue hour forecasting, and financial trends.

---

## 6.2 Key Analytics Reports

- **Patient Throughput Analysis:** Average consultation duration per doctor vs slot length.
- **Queue Efficiency Metrics:** Idle time between consultations, walk-in vs scheduled ratio.
- **Demographic Insights:** Patient distribution by age, geographic radius, and recurring diagnoses.
- **Revenue & No-Show Impact:** Financial loss calculations from un-cancelled no-shows.
